FAQ

Questions, answered

Is the eligibility scan really free?
Yes — completely. Enter your website URL and see your matched grants in 30 seconds. No sign-up, no card, no obligation. You only engage us if you decide to proceed with an application.
What types of grants can you help with?
We cover 50+ Singapore programmes across automation and digitisation, overseas expansion, R&D commercialisation, strategic hiring, workforce training, and IP development — from Enterprise SG, IMDA, WSG, and more.
How accurate is the AI matching?
The initial scan reads your public business profile and gives a strong directional view. Uploading your financials sharpens it significantly — some grants depend on revenue and headcount thresholds that can't be read from a website alone.
What happens after I click "Proceed"?
Sharon and the team reach out within one business day — usually via WhatsApp. We walk through your matched grants, agree on the best one to pursue, and take over the application end to end.
Do you only do applications, or claims too?
Both — and that's the point. Most grant money is lost after approval, when businesses fail to file claims correctly. Every engagement includes claims filing through to reimbursement.
